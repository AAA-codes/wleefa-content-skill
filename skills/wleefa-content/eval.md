# Wleefa content eval

Run this after drafting or editing. Answer each check pass or fail. Fix every failure before returning the copy.

## Wleefa rules

1. Is the copy for a known channel, and does its formality match the table in SKILL.md?
2. Does it lead with personalized 1-to-1 learning where the piece introduces Wleefa?
3. Are the terms right: tutors, learners or students, lesson, and one of the three approved call-to-action labels?
4. Is the body copy free of imperative tone, hurry words, hype, humor, and negative framing of the reader?
5. Is "Wleefa" spelled with a capital W, in US English, with sentence-case headlines?
6. No prices, no named competitors, no political or religious opinions, no guarantee wording other than "Satisfaction guaranteed"?
7. If the content concerns children, is it written to the parent?
8. Social: under 150 characters, #Wleefa plus a subject tag, 3 to 5 hashtags total?
9. Email: subject under 50 characters, no ALL CAPS, at most one emoji, one purpose, "Wleefa team" sign-off, care@wleefa.com where a support line is needed?
10. Is person consistent within the piece?

## Writing quality

11. Are the banned words, empty adverbs, and filler phrases from `writing-quality.md` gone unless quoted as examples?
12. Are binary contrasts, throat-clearing openers, faux-insight setups, colon reveals, and rhetorical setups gone?
13. Are importance puffery, weasel attribution, and superficial `-ing` analysis replaced with plain facts?
14. Are fake-profound kickers and summary-recap endings cut, so the piece ends on a concrete point or next action?
15. Is formatting plain: no emoji in headings, no decorative bold, no bullets where prose reads better, no em dashes in short copy?
16. Does every sentence earn its place, with concrete facts and direct verbs?
17. Would the copy sound natural read aloud to a colleague, with no robotic symmetry or stacked fragments?

## Output

18. For an edit, does the output include the full edited draft and a short **What changed** section?
19. Does the output end with the human-approval line?
