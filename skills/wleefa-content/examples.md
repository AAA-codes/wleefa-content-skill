# Wleefa examples: avoid and use

Approved pairs from the Wleefa Content Writer Guide. The USE column is the standard. Match its length, person, and restraint.

## Website pages

**Hero headline and subheadline**

Avoid:
> Unlock Your Potential With World-Class Tutors!
> We match you with the best teachers so you never fall behind again.

Use:
> Learn faster with a specialized tutor.
> Wleefa matches learners with certified tutors for personalized 1-to-1 online lessons.

**Pricing-style section**

Avoid:
> Lessons from just $12/hour. Hurry, spots are filling fast!

Use:
> Every learner picks a tutor, a time and a goal. Lessons are booked one at a time, no long commitment.
> Find a tutor

**Parent-facing subject page**

Avoid:
> Is your kid struggling with math? Our teachers will fix it.

Use:
> Parents choose a certified math tutor for their child, then watch progress lesson by lesson.

## Blog and SEO

**Article opening (target keyword: IELTS speaking tips)**

Avoid:
> So you've got your IELTS coming up and you're panicking about speaking? Don't worry, we've got you!

Use:
> IELTS speaking tips make a big difference in the two weeks before the test. This guide covers the five habits examiners reward most, with practice routines a learner can run alone or with a tutor.

**Comparison line**

Avoid:
> Preply is overpriced and their tutors are hit-and-miss. Wleefa is way better.

Use:
> Wleefa offers 1-to-1 online lessons. Every tutor is vetted before they join and builds each lesson around a learner's goal.

## Email

**Subject line**

Avoid:
> LAST CHANCE: 50% OFF ALL LESSONS!!! 🎉🎉🎉

Use:
> A tutor is ready for the next lesson 📚

**Re-engagement email**

Avoid:
> We noticed you haven't booked in a while. Don't let your progress slip away!

Use:
> The goal can still be updated, and so can the tutors. Wleefa's tutors pick up exactly where the last lesson ended.

**Welcome email**

Avoid:
> Hey! Welcome to the family! We're so excited you're here. Let's get you started on your journey!

Use:
> Welcome to Wleefa.
>
> We're excited to have you in Wleefa: personalized 1-on-1 lessons, Human to Human, certified tutors.
>
> Simple steps: browse certified tutors, pick one whose profile matches the goal, and book a first lesson. Satisfaction guaranteed.
>
> Questions? care@wleefa.com
> Wleefa team

## Tutor-facing

**Tutor recruiting**

Avoid:
> You'll love teaching with us! Sign up today and start earning big.
> Join our team and make money teaching online!

Use:
> Tutors on Wleefa choose their own hours, meet learners, and build a reputation lesson by lesson.
> Teach on Wleefa: set the schedule, reach learners worldwide, grow your earnings.
